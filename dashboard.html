Dataset 1 — Chicago Binary Classification
756,085 Incidents
  • Source: Chicago Police Department Open Data Portal (2021–2025).
  • Task: Report-time triage of domestic violence incidents (Domestic = 1).
  • Feature Setup: 11 Baseline inputs (14 cols) vs. 26 Domain-Engineered inputs (29 cols).
  • Champion Result: XGBoost achieves 0.9152 ROC-AUC, 87.18% Recall at τ* = 0.47.
Dataset 2 — Indian NCRB 5-Tier Forecasting
5,842 Sequences
  • Source: National Crime Records Bureau (Ministry of Home Affairs, 2001–2014).
  • Task: Sequential forecasting of district violent-crime burden across 5 fixed tiers.
  • Feature Setup: 6 Baseline crime groups vs. 11 Domain-Engineered trend features.
  • Champion Result: Enhanced LSTM achieves 72.73% Accuracy, 0.7097 Macro F1.
